EHP Hong Kong

CPD Provider No: 13882
Provides customized training solutions to give your business a competitive edge

Established since 1995, we have provided solutions for industries ranging from hotels; shipping; finance & banking; garment merchandising to logistics. We deliver training programs through a highly participative and interactive approach, making immediate connections to participants’ real job scenarios. We adopt an approach which creates for a fun learning environment thereby ensuring higher receptiveness. We train through games, role plays, case studies and other activities. To design, develop and deliver customized training solutions to enhance competitive advantage for our clients in the key areas of customer service, sales & sales management, marketing, supervisory, management and personal skill development. Our focus is always to help you develop your most important asset – your people!
